Paper Examiner

A private, local-first workspace for evaluating a complete question paper and submitted solution with your own AI provider.

How it works

1. Add the paper. Put the complete original question material into Question Paper. Use photos, text, or a mixture.

2. Add your solution. Put your complete solved work into Your Solution. You do not need to split it question-by-question.

3. Reconstruct. The AI reconstructs question numbers, parts, marks, and answer relationships from the raw material.

4. Independent review. Multiple independent reviewer roles assess the work separately.

5. Adjudicate. A Main Agent compares the reviewers' reasoning and evidence. It does not simply average their scores.

6. Report. You receive marks, percentage, question-level findings, strengths, weaknesses, disagreements, and actionable advice.

Privacy model

Paper Examiner is designed as a client-side/local-first app. Your paper files are stored locally in the browser. When you run an examination, the selected AI provider receives the material required for that request. Paper Examiner does not need its own application backend for the evaluation pipeline.

BYOK

Bring your own API key. Choose a supported provider, enter the exact model ID, and save the configuration locally. Clear site data to remove local configuration.

Supported input

Both sides can contain text, one or more images, or mixed content. Items can be reordered before evaluation.

Important

AI marking is an automated assessment, not an official examiner's result. Review the evidence and question-level reasoning, especially when handwriting, images, ambiguous questions, or marking schemes are unclear.
